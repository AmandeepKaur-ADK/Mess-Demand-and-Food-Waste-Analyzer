# Reflection — Mess Demand & Food Waste Analyser

**Name:** Amandeep Kaur

While selecting the project, I focused on identifying a real-world problem in a domain that was easily accessible to me, where I could directly interact with the people involved and obtain actual operational records. This led me to choose the college mess as my area of investigation.

Initially, I assumed that analysing historical statistics and records would be sufficient to estimate food preparation requirements. However, after interacting with the mess supervisor and staff, I realized that the process was more complex. Several external factors, such as academic holidays, examinations, college events, and menu combinations, influence food preparation decisions. This interaction changed my initial understanding of the problem and helped me approach it more practically.

AI was particularly helpful during brainstorming and problem exploration. It helped me evaluate project ideas based on accessibility, identify relevant patterns, organize my thoughts, and understand how different data points could be connected. After the fieldwork, AI also assisted in organizing the findings and structuring the project files.

However, AI sometimes introduced unnecessary suggestions while preparing documentation and writing code. I spent additional time filtering these suggestions and deciding what was actually relevant to the project's scope. If I were to approach the project again, I would simplify the process further and focus only on the essential features that directly address the identified problem.

The resulting tool is relatively small in scale and is based on just 25 days of operational data. Although it has several limitations and considerable room for improvement, I believe the underlying idea has the potential to be expanded and applied more widely with additional data and refinement.
